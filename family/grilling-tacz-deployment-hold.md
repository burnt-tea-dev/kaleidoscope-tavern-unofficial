# Historical G206 retention and its replacement

The temporary G206 hold is released for subsequent canonical candidates by
`upstream.lock.json` → `deployment_holds`. Core0.23 now has a reviewed
[G207/TACZ20011 descriptor](https://github.com/burnt-tea-dev/senluo-bedrock-core/blob/a72d6bfc5132c841454da1627a033254c4da1ab1/adapters/tacz-1.0.3-grilling-2.8.207.json)
and the matching IE20003 pair. G207 adds package notices and advances the
paired manifests; its gameplay payloads match G206. The current composition
source also skips actor-dependent oil updates in UI previews.

Earlier TACZ/IE descriptors required the exact G206 pair. Those immutable
outputs and their historical receipts remain valid for their recorded scope.
This metadata update does not replace an installed pack, upgrade a saved world,
or establish client acceptance. A new complete candidate must pass source CI,
static identity/dependency checks, one stopped-save first load/restart, actual
fault rollback and admission before LIVE adoption.
