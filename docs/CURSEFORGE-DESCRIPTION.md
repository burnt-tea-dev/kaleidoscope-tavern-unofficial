# Kaleidoscope Tavern (Unofficial)

Bring brewing, cocktails and tavern furniture to your Bedrock world. This is an unofficial port of Kaleidoscope Tavern, designed as a companion to [Kaleidoscope Cookery (Unofficial)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/kaleidoscope-cookery-unofficial).

## From grapes to cocktails

Grow grapes, press fruit, brew drinks in barrels and let them mature. Fill and display bottles, arrange racks and cabinets, and mix cocktails with a shaker. Signature cocktails combine the colors of their ingredients.

Build a tavern with bars, seating, lighting, paintings, incense and editable boards. Open Cookery's Guidebook to find Tavern's recipes, instructions and drink information together.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock 26.50+.
- Kaleidoscope Cookery (Unofficial) 1.0.6, downloaded separately.
- Enable Tavern's behavior and resource packs above Cookery.

## Status

0.6.29 is a release candidate. Full mobile visual acceptance is pending. Slightly Tipsy, Grass Stealth and Long Reach are not currently implemented. Do not label this candidate as a complete Java-parity release.
