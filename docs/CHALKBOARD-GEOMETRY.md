# Chalkboard narrow-edge source contract

The canonical owner is the eight `runtime/RP/models/entity/java_chalk_*.geo.json` resources, consumed by `runtime/BP/blocks/chalkboard.json`. `history/pre-release-0.6.29/tools/rebuild_java_wall_decor.py` is archived history, not an active exporter or release input. The current board rendering generator owns glyphs, not these bodies. No generic decoder, painting model, sandwich model or text generator is changed.

The repair adopts the reviewed candidate exactly: seven changed JSON resources and the byte-identical large center-upper control. Both small slices exchange east/west face dictionaries. Large panel 0 moves its west face to east; panel 2 moves east to west. This restores exterior X±24 caps rather than internal X±8 planes in the independently decoded north model frame. Four lower slices change only down.uv[1] from 0 to 1 and down.uv_size[1] from 1 to -1. All dimensions, origins, pivots, rotations, identifiers, broad/up faces, placements and materials remain unchanged.

`tools/chalkboard_geometry.py` reconstructs pinned Java ModelPart polygons and the renderer transform, then decodes the actual current resources using the official Bedrock origin-X and up/down endpoint rules. It checks every slice in north and east, matching each vertex's directed UV against one source exterior face and checking full exterior area. Texture bindings, current consumer ownership, source model/renderer identity and material properties are also checked. It does not infer orientation from screen-left/right or introduce a compensating facing flip. Exact input bytes and upstream freshness are recorded under `docs/evidence/chalkboard-integration-20261109/`.

This domain now enters the current resource-contract path in historical preservation checks, rather than reversing the repair or accepting a new hash ledger. No existing chalk entries were present in the functional/asset delta ledgers or launch reviewed-change lists; the obsolete comparison was the launch baseline's generic unchanged-resource comparison. The contract explicitly rejects attempts to reclaim these paths in those ledgers. Identity/history checks remain separate.

Baseline imports remain standard-library-only. Forge and Neo PNG encodings differ, while their RGBA pixels agree. The explicit pixel test imports Pillow at its processing boundary; the resource contract checks pinned bytes and the runtime's Neo texture identity without an eager pixel dependency. Mutation regressions cover every face coordinate, incorrect cap location, directed bottom UV, geometry/pivots, malformed numbers, material/route drift and both cardinal representatives.

## Evidence and limits

The original N05 editor review covers all 14 sandwich bodies, three Allium placed angles, and small/large chalk north/east. The separate repair review contains 36 model screenshots in 12 SOURCE/BEFORE/AFTER same-camera triplets. Large boards use both raking ends in north/east, exposing the missing exterior caps before repair. Small boards use both full-height end strips in north/east, exposing the wrong texels before repair. Broad front/back controls retain orientation and layout. Near-cap raking captures intentionally crop distant portions and are not whole-board framing claims.

The down-face V reversal is source-coordinate evidence only. It samples the same single texel row under nearest filtering and is not visually distinguishable. Raw JPEGs, failed diagnostics and the renderer recovery record are retained without editing. The renderer failure also affected Champagne and is not classified as an asset defect.

No native engine culling, alpha-test lighting, text/font/alignment, state transitions, waterlogging, held-view or multiplayer acceptance is claimed. Board left/center/right, legacy top placement, justified/distributed and vertical alignment extensions, all sandwich bodies, the accepted +6 tilted-rack first-person lift, and unresolved Allium held clipping remain untouched. N06 text scoping/white-atlas findings and N07 painting UV findings are separate. No Minecraft/BDS/LIVE or remote publication occurs during this local integration.
